Content OS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who uses Content OS

Content OS is a local desktop application used only by its owner, to manage his own YouTube channels, Instagram professional accounts and TikTok accounts. It has no other users and it is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the owner connects one of his accounts, Content OS receives from the platform, with his consent:

  • Account information: the account or channel ID, name, username and profile picture.
  • Statistics: follower or subscriber counts and, for each published video or post, views, likes, comments and, on YouTube, watch time.
  • Access tokens: the OAuth tokens that let the application act on the account.

It uses the publishing permissions only to upload, schedule and publish videos that the owner has approved, and to update the YouTube channel banner and description when he asks.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Access tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) and kept outside the application folder. Statistics are kept in local files. Content OS has no server and sends this data to no one other than the platform it came from.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • We do not use data for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.
  • We do not access any account other than the owner's own.

Google user data

Content OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Content OS uses the YouTube API Services; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can disconnect any account inside Content OS, which revokes the token on the platform and deletes it from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, the TikTok app settings or the Meta business integrations settings.
