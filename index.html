Content OS

A private, local publishing desk for the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What it is

Content OS is a desktop application that runs only on its owner's computer. It is not a public service and it has no other users. Its owner uses it to prepare videos for the channels he runs and to publish them, one by one, after he approves each one on screen.

What it does with each platform

  • YouTube: reads the owner's channel information and video statistics, uploads videos that the owner has approved, schedules them, sets their thumbnails and, when the owner asks, updates the channel banner and description.
  • Instagram: reads the owner's professional account information and post statistics, and publishes Reels that the owner has approved.
  • TikTok: reads the owner's account information and video statistics, and sends videos that the owner has approved to his TikTok account.

Nothing is posted without the owner's explicit approval of that specific video on that specific account.
